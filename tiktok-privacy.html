Privacy Policy — Golden Goose Publisher

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Golden Goose Publisher ("the App") is a personal content-publishing tool operated by Daniel Hernandez ("the Operator") for publishing the Operator's own video content to the Operator's own TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access via TikTok

When the Operator authorizes the App through TikTok Login, the App receives an OAuth access token and refresh token with the scopes video.upload and video.publish. The App uses these tokens solely to upload and publish videos the Operator has prepared, to the TikTok accounts the Operator has authorized. The App does not read the Operator's TikTok messages, followers, analytics, or any other account data, and does not access any other person's TikTok data.

Data we store

OAuth tokens are stored on the Operator's own private server with restricted file permissions. Video files prepared for publishing are stored on the same server. No TikTok data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the Operator revokes access (in TikTok's authorized-apps settings) or deletes them from the server. Revoking access in TikTok immediately stops the App from acting on the account. To request deletion of any stored data, contact the Operator at the email below.

Children

The App is operated by and for an adult and is not directed at children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the App evolves; the date above reflects the latest revision.
