How does call forwarding work?

Your phone rings first. If you don’t answer, your provider forwards the call to the AI receptionist. We help you configure conditional forwarding and test it with you. Availability and any forwarding charges depend on your phone provider.

Do I keep my business number?

Yes. Customers keep calling the number they already know. No number change or porting is needed. A separate routing number works behind the scenes.

Can it book into my calendar?

Yes, with a supported calendar connection and the booking rules you approve. We check your booking platform before setup, then test availability, time zones, and appointment creation before calls go live.

What if the AI can’t help someone?

It captures the caller’s name, callback details, and request for you to follow up. It follows your approved instructions instead of guessing prices or promising work. It is not an emergency service.

What do the free 30 days include?

The same service: answering forwarded calls, booking through a supported calendar, and texting you the details. Setup is free. Your trial starts when your tested setup goes live. We confirm compatibility and expected call volume before activation.

How do I cancel?

Email inboxhandledai@gmail.com before your next renewal to stop future billing. There is no long-term contract or cancellation fee. We help you turn off forwarding so calls return to your normal phone setup.
