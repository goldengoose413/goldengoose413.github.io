🏰 Castle Saved!

How to play

Build walls to lock the Golden Goose, its friends and the towers safely inside the castle — and keep every red invader outside.

1️⃣ Tap a wall, then tap a square to place it.
2️⃣ Tap a selected wall again to turn it before placing.
3️⃣ Tap a placed wall to turn it, or tap its chip to pick it back up.
4️⃣ Press Check the Walls when all walls are placed.

Each level has exactly one correct answer. Good luck, guardian! 🪿
